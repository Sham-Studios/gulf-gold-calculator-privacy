Frequently asked questions

Where do the gold rates come from?
For the UAE, the app uses the Dubai jewellery board rate. For Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman it shows an estimated jeweller board rate based on the gold price, the exchange rate and a typical premium. Rates refresh about every 15 minutes.

Why is the shop's rate different from the app?
Shops set their own rates and fees. Use the shop-rate comparison in the app to see the difference.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, or manage your subscription in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Are the results financial advice?
No. Results are estimates. Always confirm the final price with the shop.
