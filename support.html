Frequently asked questions

Where do the gold rates come from?
For the UAE, the app uses the Dubai jewellery board rate. For Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman it shows the local jeweller board rate, published daily by a major Gulf jeweller. If that board can't be read, it follows the international gold price at the official exchange rate. Rates refresh about every 15 minutes.

Why is the shop's rate different from the app?
Shops set their own rates and fees. Use the shop-rate comparison in the app to see the difference.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. To manage or cancel your subscription on iPhone, go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Are the results financial advice?
No. Results are estimates. Always confirm the final price with the shop.
