Gulf Gold Calculator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Gulf Gold Calculator ("the app") helps you check how much of a quoted gold price is gold, VAT and the jeweller's making fee. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Anonymous usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is used, for example which screens are opened, which country and karat are selected, and whether a price check was run. This data is tied only to a random identifier for the app on your device, not to your name, email address or any account. If you subscribe, that identifier is also linked to your subscription record, which we use to understand how purchases relate to app use. The app does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. See Google's privacy policy.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Information stored on your device

Your choices (language, country, purpose, karat and any custom VAT rate) are stored only on your device so the app can remember them. This information is not sent to us.

Gold prices

To show current prices, the app downloads a rates file from our own rates service, which is hosted by Cloudflare. If that service is unavailable, the app requests public gold-rate data directly from third-party rate sources instead. These requests are standard network requests and may expose your IP address to Cloudflare or to those sources; Cloudflare may keep ordinary server logs. The rates file is the same for everyone: it contains only prices, and the service receives no information about you or your use of the calculator. The numbers you type into the calculator never leave your device.

Subscriptions

The app requires an auto-renewing subscription. Payments are processed by the store you got the app from: by Apple through your Apple ID on iPhone, or by Google through your Google account on Android. We never see your payment details. To manage subscriptions and unlock access, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-user identifier, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy. You can cancel any time. On iPhone, go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
